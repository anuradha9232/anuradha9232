## Hi, I'm Anuradha

I work in AI training and data annotation - designing evaluation rubrics, writing adversarial test cases, and checking model outputs against verified ground truth. A lot of what I do comes down to one thing: turning "this answer feels wrong" into clear, weighted criteria that anyone can apply and get the same result. That is really quality assurance, and it is the part of model evaluation I enjoy most.

Before this I spent years keeping the books for a family business - accounts, invoicing, and GST records - so I am comfortable with messy data, reconciliation, and getting numbers exactly right.

### What I do

- **Rubric design & model evaluation** - weighted positive/negative criteria, pass/fail scoring, QA methodology
- **Adversarial testing** - prompts built to surface the mistakes models actually make
- **Data annotation & labeling** - writing guidelines, labeling, and measuring inter-annotator agreement
- **Data & analytics** - advanced Excel, Power BI, data cleaning, and reporting
- **Finance domain** - accounting, GST, and invoice/financial-data validation
