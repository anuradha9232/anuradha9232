### About me

I work in **AI training and model evaluation** - designing the rubrics and test cases that decide whether a model's answer is actually correct. Most of what I do comes down to one skill: turning *"this answer feels wrong"* into clear, weighted, pass/fail criteria that any reviewer can apply and reach the same score. That is quality assurance applied to AI, and it is the part I enjoy most.

At **Outlier** I have worked across six projects - designing evaluation rubrics, writing adversarial prompts that surface model failures, verifying ground-truth answers from source data, and evaluating multimodal and agent-based tasks. Before AI training I spent years keeping the books for a family business (accounts, invoicing, GST records), which is where my eye for data accuracy and reconciliation comes from - and a domain edge for finance data work.
